Privacy

How DRMOHAMED.NET handles personal information: what is processed, why, how long it is kept and the rights every visitor holds.

Privacy notice in brief

This notice explains, in plain language, what personal information this platform processes, why it is processed and the rights every visitor holds.

DRMOHAMED.NET collects only what it needs to operate. The main categories are the details you choose to send through the contact form, limited technical logs that keep the website secure, your language choice stored in your own browser, a record of the choices you make about optional processing and, when the Yaqoot guided service is enabled, conversation data handled as described below.

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time through the contact form. The sections that follow give the full detail.

Who is responsible for your information

DRMOHAMED.NET is the official publishing platform of Dr. Mohamed Yousef Almadfaei. It is responsible for the personal information processed on this website. Nothing on this platform represents the views of any former or current public employer, and no such employer has any role in the handling of your information.

The way to reach the platform about any privacy matter is the contact form on this website.

The information this platform may process

  • Contact-form information: your name, email address, organisation if you choose to give it, subject and message, together with your consent to be answered.
  • Limited technical logs: the routine records the servers keep to deliver pages, prevent misuse and keep the website secure.
  • Language preference: your chosen language, stored only in your own browser so the site opens the same way on your next visit.
  • Consent records: a record of any optional processing choices you make, so the platform can honour them.
  • Yaqoot conversation data: the questions and answers exchanged with the AI guide, handled according to the final service configuration described in this notice and the Responsible AI notice.

Why it is used and on what basis

Contact information is used only to assess and respond to your stated enquiry. It is not used for marketing, and marketing consent is never bundled with an enquiry. Where consent is the basis for processing, the form asks for it through a clear, unchecked control.

Technical logs are processed because a website cannot be delivered or kept secure without them. The language preference exists only to honour your choice. Yaqoot conversations are processed to provide the guided service and to meet controlled operational needs.

The platform's review baseline for this processing is UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 concerning the protection of personal data.

Who receives it and where it may go

Your information is not sold and is not shared for advertising. A small number of service providers act for the platform by function or approved category, for example hosting, security and enquiry handling. Each provider is bound to process information only for the platform's stated purpose.

Some providers may operate outside the United Arab Emirates, which means information may cross borders. Providers are engaged by function or approved category, and each is bound to process information only for the platform's stated purpose.

How long it is kept

Nothing is kept longer than its purpose requires.

Your language preference is stored only in your own browser and remains under your control. Operational logs are retained briefly and then deleted or anonymised. Whether Yaqoot conversation text is retained, and whether it may ever be used for improvement, will be stated plainly in the published notice once the production configuration is fixed.

How it is protected

The platform applies proportionate technical and organisational measures: restricted access, protected submission handling and separation between diagnostic logs and content analytics. Access to exceptional submissions, such as a message containing sensitive information, is restricted, and such material is not reused.

The detailed security controls are maintained in the platform's technical record and are not published, so that publishing them cannot itself create a risk.

Your rights

  • Access: ask whether the platform holds information about you and receive a copy.
  • Correction: ask for inaccurate or incomplete information to be put right.
  • Deletion: ask for your information to be erased where there is no continuing need to keep it.
  • Restriction: ask for processing to be limited while a question is resolved.
  • Objection: object to a particular use of your information.

How to exercise your rights or raise a concern

Send your request through the contact form and it will reach a monitored human channel. Identity is verified using the minimum necessary evidence, so that information is never released to an impostor, and every request is tracked to completion.

If you believe your information has been mishandled, you may first raise the concern through the same route so it can be put right. You also have the right to complain to the competent supervisory authority in the United Arab Emirates.

Effective date and changes

This notice was issued on 26 July 2026. If the way the platform processes information changes in a material way, the updated notice will be published on this page with a new date before the change takes effect.
