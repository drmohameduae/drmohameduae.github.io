Biography

Executive Profile

H.E. Dr. Mohamed Yousef Almadfaei is an Emirati strategic adviser and environmental governance leader with more than two decades of experience in public policy, strategic planning, environmental management, institutional transformation, and security and strategic affairs.

He is the Founder and Chairman of Shura Global Consultancy, established in London in 2020 with its operational headquarters in Abu Dhabi. He previously served as Director General of the Environment Protection and Development Authority in Ras Al Khaimah and as Executive Director of the Integrated Environmental Policy and Planning Sector at the Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi.

He brings together public-sector leadership, policy development, international cooperation, and strategic advisory work, with a continuing focus on making environmental governance a driver of economic development, climate resilience, and quality of life.

Shura Global Consultancy

As Founder and Chairman, he leads the firm’s strategic advisory, policy formulation, research, and partnership work for government entities and institutions in the UAE and internationally.

The firm focuses on sustainability, governance, public policy, institutional transformation, strategic planning, public-private partnerships, investment facilitation, and international cooperation. Under his leadership, Shura Global Consultancy has pursued the responsible use of artificial intelligence in advisory work and explored solutions in climate finance, carbon markets, and emerging technologies.

Through the firm, he supports institutions seeking clearer strategy, stronger governance, and meaningful, measurable, and sustainable impact.

Environment Protection and Development Authority

In 2026, he was appointed Director General of the Environment Protection and Development Authority in Ras Al Khaimah by His Highness Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi, Member of the UAE Supreme Council and Ruler of Ras Al Khaimah. He was entrusted with leading the Authority’s work on environmental protection, sustainable development, biodiversity conservation, environmental regulation and compliance, and climate resilience.

He led the development of a three-year institutional transformation programme designed to position EPDA as a globally leading AI-powered environmental authority. The programme centred on an integrated Environmental Intelligence Ecosystem bringing together real-time environmental monitoring, artificial intelligence analytics, geographic information systems, digital-twin capabilities, and data governance to modernise permitting, inspections, compliance, and evidence-led environmental enforcement.

He also established a pathway towards carbon-neutral, paperless operations aligned with the UAE Net Zero by 2050 Strategic Initiative. This included strategic directions for carbon accounting, emissions measurement and disclosure, climate finance, and a carbon-credit and carbon-market platform intended to support emissions reduction and green investment.

Within this agenda, he worked on setting up a partnership with the Global Carbon Council to explore carbon-credit standards, carbon-market opportunities, and institutional readiness for participation in the low-carbon economy.

Capacity building and national-talent development were integral to the transformation agenda, with emphasis on strengthening capabilities in environmental governance, digital services, artificial intelligence, data management, environmental monitoring, and compliance.

His work also covered digital regulatory services, stronger governance and delegation arrangements, a more effective operating model, and sustainable marine-development initiatives. These included looking into the Blue Ports Initiative for fishermen’s ports, incorporating smart fish-landing monitoring, vessel tracking, electronic catch registration, renewable energy, waste management, and marine-biodiversity protection.

Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi

He spent more than 17 years at the Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi between 2002 and 2019, progressing through technical and leadership roles, including Director of Environmental Policy and Planning, before serving as Executive Director of the Integrated Environmental Policy and Planning Sector.

He was a key contributor to the development and launch of the Abu Dhabi Environment Vision 2030 and led the development of the Emirate’s General Environmental Policy and Environmental Policy Development Framework. He also contributed to the environmental pillar of UAE Vision 2021 and introduced the UAE Water Budget concept, which was later integrated into UAE Vision 2021 initiatives.

He led the development of Abu Dhabi’s Single-Use Plastic Bag Policy as part of a wider effort to reduce plastic pollution, promote responsible consumption, and support the transition towards a circular economy.

He also led the Agency’s Digital Transformation Team and oversaw the development and launch of the Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi’s first mobile application. He further led an operational-effectiveness review across EAD to streamline processes, strengthen service delivery, improve integration across systems and functions, and enhance institutional performance.

He made capacity building and Emiratisation a priority, developing Emirati talent in environmental policy, planning, and institutional leadership. During that period, his sector recorded the highest Emiratisation rate among EAD’s sectors, while several professionals he mentored progressed to senior local and federal leadership positions.

He also oversaw the development and publication of the Abu Dhabi State of Environment Report 2018. It was produced in-house for the first time by a team consisting primarily of young Emirati nationals and subject-matter experts.

His responsibilities included aligning government programmes with environmental priorities and contributing to strategies addressing water resources management, climate change, biodiversity, air quality, marine water quality, and integrated waste management.

His operational contributions included the development of Abu Dhabi’s environmental inspection system, chemical and hazardous-materials monitoring mechanism, and related Code of Practice. He also led work with government partners on economic-environmental integration, including the economic valuation of Abu Dhabi’s groundwater resources.

Regional and International Engagement

He contributed to the launch of the Abu Dhabi Global Environmental Data Initiative (AGEDI) at the World Summit on Sustainable Development in Johannesburg in 2002. He also led or contributed to the Unified GCC Water Resources Management Strategy, the UAE Water Budget Concept, and the UAE General Policy on Climate Change.

He has represented the UAE at international conferences, led environmental coordination across local, federal, regional, and international stakeholders, and contributed to major global gatherings on oceans, the green economy, and clean air.

Community Engagement

He supports initiatives that promote the inclusion and empowerment of People of Determination, educational advancement, human-capital development, and humanitarian, charitable, and community action in the UAE.
