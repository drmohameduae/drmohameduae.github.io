Biography

Executive Profile

H.E. Dr. Mohamed Yousef Almadfaei is an Emirati strategic adviser and environmental governance leader with more than two decades of experience in public policy, strategic planning, environmental management, institutional transformation, and security and strategic affairs.

He is the Founder and Chairman of Shura Global Consultancy, established in London in 2020 with its operational headquarters in Abu Dhabi. He previously served as Director General of the Environment Protection and Development Authority in Ras Al Khaimah and as Executive Director of the Integrated Environmental Policy and Planning Sector at the Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi.

He brings together public-sector leadership, policy development, international cooperation, and strategic advisory work, with a continuing focus on making environmental governance a driver of economic development, climate resilience, and quality of life.

Shura Global Consultancy

As Founder and Chairman, he leads the firm’s strategic advisory, policy formulation, research, and partnership work for government entities and institutions in the UAE and internationally.

The firm focuses on sustainability, governance, public policy, institutional transformation, strategic planning, public-private partnerships, investment facilitation, and international cooperation. Under his leadership, Shura Global Consultancy has pursued the responsible use of artificial intelligence in advisory work and explored solutions in climate finance, carbon markets, and emerging technologies.

Through the firm, he supports institutions seeking clearer strategy, stronger governance, and meaningful, measurable, and sustainable impact.

Environment Protection and Development Authority

In 2026, he was appointed Director General of the Environment Protection and Development Authority in Ras Al Khaimah by His Highness Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi, Member of the UAE Supreme Council and Ruler of Ras Al Khaimah, and was entrusted with leading the Authority’s work on environmental protection, sustainable development, biodiversity conservation, environmental regulation and compliance, and climate resilience.

He led the development of a three-year institutional transformation programme to position EPDA as a globally leading, AI-powered environmental authority — centred on an integrated Environmental Intelligence Ecosystem bringing together real-time monitoring, artificial-intelligence analytics, geographic information systems and digital-twin capabilities to modernise permitting, inspections, compliance and enforcement.

He also set the Authority on a pathway towards carbon-neutral, paperless operations aligned with the UAE Net Zero by 2050 Strategic Initiative, including a partnership with the Global Carbon Council on carbon-credit standards and carbon-market readiness, alongside capacity building, national-talent development and sustainable marine-development initiatives for the fishermen’s ports of Ras Al Khaimah.

Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi

He spent more than 17 years at the Environment Agency – Abu Dhabi between 2002 and 2019, progressing through technical and leadership roles to serve as Executive Director of the Integrated Environmental Policy and Planning Sector.

He was a key contributor to the Abu Dhabi Environment Vision 2030, led the Emirate’s General Environmental Policy and Environmental Policy Development Framework, contributed to the environmental pillar of UAE Vision 2021, and introduced the UAE Water Budget concept later integrated into national initiatives.

He led the development of Abu Dhabi’s Single-Use Plastic Bag Policy, headed the Agency’s Digital Transformation Team and launched its first mobile application, and oversaw the Abu Dhabi State of Environment Report 2018 — produced in-house for the first time by a team of young Emirati nationals and subject-matter experts.

He made capacity building and Emiratisation a priority: during his tenure, his sector recorded the highest Emiratisation rate among EAD’s sectors, and several professionals he mentored progressed to senior local and federal leadership positions.

Selected Global Platforms

Across his career, he has represented the UAE and contributed to leading global platforms, including the World Summit on Sustainable Development, the World Government Summit, the UN Water Conference, Abu Dhabi Sustainability Week, The Economist Enterprise Events, the World Ocean Summit, the World Water Forum, and the UN Climate Change Conferences (COP).

He also contributed to the Unified GCC Water Resources Management Strategy and the UAE General Policy on Climate Change, and led environmental coordination across local, federal, regional and international stakeholders.

Community Engagement

He supports initiatives that promote the inclusion and empowerment of People of Determination, educational advancement, human-capital development, and humanitarian, charitable, and community action in the UAE.
