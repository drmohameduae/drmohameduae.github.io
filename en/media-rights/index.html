Media Rights

How copyright, licensing, consent and dignity govern the text, poetry, photographs and monogram published on DRMOHAMED.NET.

Media rights on this platform

Every substantive asset on DRMOHAMED.NET carries a recorded ownership or licence status before it is published.

This page explains how the platform protects original work, how third-party material is cleared and how consent and dignity govern photographs of people. It also gives the route for rights enquiries and image-removal requests.

Original works

Copyright applies to the original website text, manuscripts, poetry, translations, speeches, photographs, videos, graphics and the monogram published here, according to ownership or licence. Poetry and translations are treated as distinct rights-bearing works in their own right.

The platform's review baseline for these matters is UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2021 on copyright and neighbouring rights. Unless a page states otherwise, the copyright line is: © 2026 Dr. Mohamed Yousef Almadfaei. All rights reserved. Short quotation with attribution is permitted as described in the Terms of Use.

Third-party material

Third-party material is published only with a recorded licence, permission, public-domain status or another valid legal basis, documented for the intended context, duration and territory. Attribution alone does not create permission, and owning a print or a file does not by itself establish copyright permission.

An ownership and licence record is maintained for every substantive asset, including creator, owner, subjects, permitted channels, credit form and any restrictions. Credits on the page match the terms of the underlying agreement.

Consent, dignity and context

Legal permission is necessary but not sufficient on this platform. Images involving dignitaries, public events, colleagues, family members and children receive context-sensitive review, and a dignity concern may justify not publishing even where a legal right exists. Public availability elsewhere does not automatically make an image appropriate for permanent republication here.

Captions state only verified context: dignitaries and roles are named accurately for the event date. Family material is published only with explicit authority and without exposing private details, and children are not identified beyond what is necessary and specifically authorised. Consent and copyright are treated as separate questions, each answered on the record.

No implied endorsement

A photograph of the Founder with any person or institution does not imply partnership, endorsement, current association or institutional approval, and captions are written so that proximity cannot be read as a testimonial. Trademarks and institutional logos are used only with authority.

Metadata

Location or device metadata that could create a risk is removed from media before publication. No private address, schedule or sensitive location is exposed through the files this website serves.

Rights enquiries and removal requests

Rights enquiries, licensing questions, infringement notices and image-removal requests all begin at the contact form on this website. The route is monitored and tested, and removal requests are handled respectfully through the platform's legal workflow, with each decision recorded.

If you appear in an image and would like it reviewed or removed, say so plainly in your message. You do not need to make a legal argument for the request to be considered.

Status of this notice

This page is the Version 1.0 drafting baseline issued 26 July 2026. The final wording is subject to approval by qualified UAE counsel before public launch, and it will be updated if the production configuration changes.
