The Journey

A sequence of learning and contribution across environmental governance, public policy and institutional development.

The timeline is a sequence of learning and contribution. Dates and titles support the story; they do not become the story.

  1. Roots and Values

    From the earliest years, two threads remained constant: a quiet sense of duty and a belief that meaning is found in action rather than rhetoric. Honesty, service and generosity were not presented as theories; they were demonstrated through daily life in the UAE.

  2. Academic Formation

    Study in the UAE, the United Kingdom and the United States deepened an understanding of governance, strategy and the interdependence of environmental and social systems. Academic qualifications include a PhD from the University of Reading, an MSc from the UAE National Defense College and a BSc from the University of St. Thomas.

  3. Environmental Leadership

    At the Environment Agency - Abu Dhabi, responsibility grew across whole-of-government policy and planning for waste, water, biodiversity, climate change and air quality. The work included major policy frameworks and collaborative national and regional strategies.

  4. Institutional Leadership and Shura

    Later leadership in Ras Al Khaimah reinforced the value of translating policy into practical institutional systems. Shura Global Consultancy now extends that commitment through dialogue, evidence and shared wisdom - the meaning carried by the name Shura.
