A journey shaped by curiosity, public service and the responsibility to leave institutions stronger than they were found.

Dr. Mohamed Yousef Almadfaei is an Emirati environmental leader, strategic adviser and institution-builder whose work has connected policy, sustainability, governance and long-term national development.

His experience spans environmental policy, government strategy, organisational transformation, institutional excellence and the responsible use of emerging technology. He formerly served in senior leadership roles at the Environment Agency - Abu Dhabi and as Director General of the Environment Protection and Development Authority in Ras Al Khaimah.

Today, as Founder and Chairman of Shura Global Consultancy, he supports public and private institutions seeking clearer strategy, stronger governance and meaningful, sustainable impact.

Areas of Expertise

  • Environmental governance and sustainability

  • Strategic planning and public policy

  • Institutional development and government excellence

  • Organisational transformation

  • Artificial intelligence strategy and ethics

  • Leadership and public administration
